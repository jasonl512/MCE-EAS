Energy score method using real Manhattan data

Recommended current method: use the property-level ENERGY STAR score reported in the NYC LL84 extract when available. Leave the score blank when unavailable.

Why this replaces the proposed EUI min-max conversion

Site EUI describes energy use per square foot, but offices, hotels, schools, and housing operate differently. One Manhattan-wide minimum and maximum would compare unlike properties. The ENERGY STAR score is already a 1-100 comparison for eligible properties and is designed to compare a property with similar buildings while accounting for relevant operating and climate characteristics.

The proposed 5th/95th percentile EUI conversion is not used as a final energy score. Percentiles can flag records for review, but do not by themselves justify an efficiency score.
