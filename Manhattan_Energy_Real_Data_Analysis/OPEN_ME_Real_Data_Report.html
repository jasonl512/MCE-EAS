Manhattan building energy: real-data analysis

Calendar year 2024 · NYC Local Law 84 reporting-property records · Preliminary descriptive results

No synthetic inputs, normalized energy scores, or Green Scores are used in this analysis. The results describe a conservatively screened subset of the repository extract. They do not represent all Manhattan buildings or establish district efficiency rankings.

The initial 1,400 exclusions include 1,360 missing Site EUI values, 28 nonpositive values, and 12 invalid community-board codes. Flags may overlap in general; exclusions are counted once per record. Missing values stay missing.

For this preliminary analysis, we set aside records with parent links, records referenced as parents elsewhere in this extract, or physical BBL/BIN identifiers shared with another property ID. These are unresolved relationships, not confirmed errors or duplicates. This conservative screen reduces coverage and may systematically exclude campuses and complex properties.

We then set aside otherwise eligible records with Site EUI below 5 or above 1,000, following NYC DOB's advice to investigate those values. This is a data-review screen, not a Green Score conversion range. All excluded records and reasons are retained in record_audit.csv.

2. What do the real values show?

Multifamily Housing: median 69.3, n=5,220. Office: median 59.2, n=1,245. Hotel: median 90.5, n=311. These differences describe reported energy use per floor area; they do not establish that one property type is more efficient.

The distribution plot includes all screened records on a logarithmic horizontal axis. Property-type lines show the interquartile range, not confidence intervals. The eight most represented types with at least 30 records are shown; the full table includes every type.

5. Remaining reviews and sensitivity

Within each property type with at least 20 included records and a positive interquartile range, values beyond Q1 − 1.5×IQR or Q3 + 1.5×IQR are flagged for review. 301 screened records receive this statistical flag and remain in the primary results. The threshold is an exploratory analyst choice, not a validated error test.
